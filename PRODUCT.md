# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated by the autonomous build instruction. Vite and browser JavaScript; PDF.js for local document rendering. Deployment on Vercel.
## Users
Construction site engineers checking room areas and takeoffs; Junction judges assessing evidence and correctness.
## Product purpose
Answer a narrow set of quantity questions with inspectable geometry and allow a person to trace unsupported drawings with an explicit scale.
## Capabilities and constraints
All code authored during Junction 2026. Public MIT code; third-party drawings retain their licenses and stay outside Git. No fabricated human validation, inference results or confidence scores. DXF semantic layers are an easier subset than raw scans. Manual boundaries require human review.
## Evidence on hand
Local official preview with 7,990 questions. Swiss Dwellings DXFs contain metre-unit room polygons, labels and printed areas. No independent human ground truth has been collected.
## Product principles
Compute quantities from coordinates. Show the exact boundary. Refuse an unsupported request. Separate printed-area checks from independent validation.
